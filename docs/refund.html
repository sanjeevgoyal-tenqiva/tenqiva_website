Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines the terms under which Tenqiva ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") processes refunds and cancellations across both of its divisions — Tenqiva Software and Tenqiva Experiences — for services and products purchased through our website tenqiva.com ("Website"). This policy is published in compliance with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines for Payment Aggregators.

1. SaaS Products & Subscriptions

1.1 Free Trial

Where offered, free trials do not require payment information. At the end of the trial period, you may choose to subscribe or discontinue use. No charges are incurred during the trial period.

1.2 Cancellation of Subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time by contacting us at billing@tenqiva.com or through your account dashboard. Upon cancellation:

  • Your access to the paid features will continue until the end of the current billing cycle.
  • No further charges will be applied after cancellation.
  • We do not provide prorated refunds for partial billing periods unless specified otherwise below.

2. Consulting Services

2.1 Cancellation

Consulting engagements are governed by a Statement of Work (SOW) or service agreement. Cancellation terms are as follows:

  • Before project commencement: Full refund of any advance payment, minus a 10% administrative fee, processed within 7–10 business days.
  • After project commencement: Refund will be calculated based on the work completed and deliverables provided. A detailed breakdown will be shared within 5 business days of the cancellation request.
  • After delivery of final deliverables: No refund is applicable once final deliverables have been accepted or deemed accepted (30 days after delivery without written objection).

3. Experiences — Decoration, Events & Managed Services

Engagements delivered by Tenqiva Experiences are performed on a fixed date at a client's premises. Materials are procured, crew is rostered and dates are blocked in advance, so cancellation terms are based on how close to the scheduled date the cancellation is received. These terms apply alongside the accepted quotation or work order; where the work order states different commercial terms for a specific engagement, the work order prevails.

3.1 Booking Confirmation & Advance

A date is held only once the quotation or work order is accepted in writing and the advance stated in it is received. Until then, dates remain open to other clients. Site surveys and concept proposals are provided free of charge and carry no obligation to proceed.

Cancellation takes effect from the date we receive your written notice at billing@tenqiva.com, not the date it was sent.

3.3 Postponement

Where you postpone rather than cancel, and the new date falls within 90 days of the original and is confirmed at least 7 days before the original date, amounts already paid are carried forward in full to the new date. One postponement is accommodated at no charge; any additional outstation logistics or storage cost arising from the change is quoted to you in advance for approval. Perishable items already procured for the original date cannot be carried forward.

3.4 Cancellation or Shortfall by Tenqiva

If we cancel a confirmed engagement for any reason within our control, you receive a full refund of all amounts paid within 7 business days, and we will assist in sourcing an alternative provider where the date allows. If an agreed element of the scope is not delivered on the day, the value of that element is refunded or credited, assessed against the accepted work order.

3.5 Quality Concerns

Concerns about installation or service quality should be raised with the on-site project manager at the time, and in writing within 48 hours of the installation or event. We will re-work or rectify at no additional cost where the shortfall is attributable to us. Where rectification is not possible because the event date has passed, a proportional refund or credit is assessed against the accepted work order and settled within 15 business days.

3.6 Circumstances Beyond Reasonable Control

Where an engagement cannot proceed because of circumstances beyond either party's reasonable control — including a government restriction, a public emergency, a natural event, or withdrawal of building or landlord permission — we will first offer to reschedule at no charge. Where rescheduling is not possible, amounts paid are refunded less only those third-party costs already irrevocably committed, itemised with evidence.

3.7 Recurring Managed Services

Facility, housekeeping, pantry, catering and manpower deployments billed monthly may be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Service continues, and remains payable, through the notice period. No refund is due for months already elapsed.

4. One-Time Product Purchases

For one-time purchases (e.g., Power BI templates, digital products):

  • Before download/access: Full refund within 5–7 business days.
  • After download/access: No refund, as digital products cannot be returned. If the product is defective or substantially different from the description, a replacement or refund will be provided within 7–10 business days.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or cancellation, please contact us through any of the following channels:

  • Email: billing@tenqiva.com
  • Phone: +91-9398178943 (Monday – Saturday, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST)
  • Contact Form: Available on our Contact page

Your request must include:

  • Your full name and registered email address.
  • Order/Transaction ID or Invoice Number.
  • Reason for cancellation or refund request.
  • Supporting documentation (if applicable).

6. Refund Processing

All approved refunds will be processed as follows:

  • Mode of Refund: Refunds will be credited back to the original payment method used at the time of purchase (credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, or wallet).
  • Processing Time: Refunds are initiated within 5–7 business days of approval. The actual credit to your account may take an additional 5–10 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Currency: All refunds are processed in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • Confirmation: You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated, along with the estimated credit timeline.

7. Failed Transactions

If funds have been deducted from your account but the transaction has failed or the service was not activated:

  • The amount will be automatically refunded to your account within 5–7 business days.
  • If you do not receive the refund within 7 business days, please contact us at billing@tenqiva.com with the transaction reference number.

8. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Services that have been fully delivered and accepted.
  • Digital products that have been downloaded or accessed.
  • Subscription fees for periods that have already elapsed.
  • Customised or bespoke development work that has been completed as per agreed specifications.
  • Third-party charges, taxes, or payment gateway fees (where applicable).
  • Custom-fabricated decoration elements, printed or branded items, and perishable food and beverage stock already produced or procured for a confirmed date.
  • Engagements cancelled with less than 7 days' notice before the scheduled installation or event date.
  • Site visits, concept boards and quotations — these are provided free of charge and are never invoiced.

9. Dispute Resolution

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of a refund request, you may escalate the matter as follows:

  • Step 1: Contact our Grievance Officer at grievance@tenqiva.com. We will review and respond within 15 business days.
  • Step 2: If the matter remains unresolved, you may approach the appropriate Consumer Forum or Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission as per the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
