Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

This Shipping and Delivery Policy explains how {{COMPANY_NAME}} ("Company", "Tenqiva", "we", "us", or "our") delivers the services and products purchased through our website tenqiva.com ("Website"). This policy is published in compliance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines for Payment Aggregators and Payment Gateways.

1. Nature of Our Offerings

Tenqiva operates two divisions, and the way an order is fulfilled depends on which division it belongs to.

Division 01 — Tenqiva Software (digital, no physical shipment). These offerings are digital and are delivered electronically over the internet. No shipping charges, courier charges, freight, or physical delivery timelines apply:

  • SaaS product subscriptions — cloud-hosted software accessed through a web browser or mobile application.
  • Consulting and professional services — delivered as documentation, source code, dashboards, reports, and advisory sessions.
  • Digital products — such as Power BI templates and downloadable assets.

Division 02 — Tenqiva Experiences (on-site services performed at your premises). These are managed services carried out by our personnel at a location you nominate. They are not goods sold for dispatch to a customer address, and we do not operate a mail-order or courier-based retail channel. Any materials involved — decoration elements, installations, food and beverage, gifting items, or hired equipment — are procured by us as part of performing the service and are brought to and installed at your site by our own crew or our appointed logistics partners, at the date and time agreed in the quotation or work order. These include:

  • Decoration and ambience — festive and year-round decoration, office branding and signage, greenscaping.
  • Events and production — annual days, town halls, launches, conferences, and the associated AV and stage production.
  • Food and beverage — corporate catering, event dining, pantry and cafeteria management.
  • Workplace managed services — facility and housekeeping, front desk and guest services, manpower deployment.
  • Employee experience — engagement programmes, wellness activities, and corporate gifting, including delivery of gifting items to a corporate address you nominate.

Where an engagement includes corporate gifting or hampers delivered to one or more addresses you specify, delivery is made by us or by our appointed courier within the timeline recorded in the work order, and any applicable delivery cost is shown in the quotation before you accept it. We do not levy a separate shipping charge that is not disclosed in the quotation.

3. Delivery Confirmation

Delivery is deemed complete when:

  • Login credentials or an activation link have been sent to the email address registered at the time of purchase; or
  • A download link for a digital product has been made available to you; or
  • Agreed deliverables for a consulting engagement have been transmitted to you; or
  • For an Experiences engagement, the installation, event or service has been performed at your premises and signed off on the site completion report, or the gifting consignment has been delivered to the address you nominated.

You will receive an email confirmation together with a tax invoice for every successful transaction. Please ensure that the email address supplied at checkout is accurate and monitored, and check your spam or promotions folder if you do not see our email.

4. Service Availability

Our SaaS products are hosted on cloud infrastructure and are designed to be available on a continuous basis, subject to scheduled maintenance and events beyond our reasonable control. Scheduled maintenance is notified in advance by email or through an in-application notice wherever practicable. Extended unplanned downtime may entitle you to a credit or refund as set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6. Geographic Availability

Our digital services (Division 01 — Software) are available to customers in India and, where lawful, internationally.

Our on-site services (Division 02 — Experiences) are performed in India only. Hyderabad and the surrounding Telangana region are served directly by our own crew; other Indian cities are served on a project basis, and any travel, accommodation or outstation logistics cost is itemised in the quotation before you accept it. We do not undertake on-site engagements outside India.

All transactions are processed in Indian Rupees (INR). Customers outside India are responsible for any currency conversion charges, foreign transaction fees, or local taxes levied by their bank or jurisdiction.
